# Independent solutions revision

The public homepage now introduces power banks, portable stations, fitted backup
and solar as one independent service. Five needs-based guide cards replace the
manufacturer product rail. Their links prefill the existing calculator. The
hero, guide cards and result use five clearly labelled generic AI illustrations.
Exact manufacturer/model details are retained for quoting and order review.

The calculator retains appliance tiles, quantity/hours controls, outage hours,
budget and optional spend. Optional equipment and solar preferences sit in one
collapsed disclosure. Results can compare multiple power-safe options, retain
the selected model through checkout, or give an installer-reviewed system brief.
There is no fabricated installed-system price or promised component pairing.

The root controller now includes 50 additional sourcing records. Two verified
USB banks are enabled for enquiries with prices on request. Forty-eight new
records remain disabled pending specifications or supply checks. The New Age
records are research leads only. `SOLUTIONS_RESEARCH.md` documents the sources,
test conditions, missing data and calculation assumptions.

Validation: `node --test` passes all 122 tests in eight suites, with none skipped.
The copy lint reports zero banned technical terms in plain copy and zero
sentences over 20 words. Browser checks at 1440, 390 and 360 pixels pass phone
backup, existing equipment, editable solar estimates, WhatsApp message contents,
choice switching and actual-model checkout pricing. No browser errors or page
overflow were observed. No messages or real payments were sent during checks.

Screenshots include full-page views at 1440 and 390 pixels, plus the hero, guide,
phone result and solar result under ignored `.screenshots/solutions-*`.

Five-line visual critique:

1. The generic product family gives the hero a broader scope while retaining the centred composition.
2. A local dark gradient improves headline contrast while leaving the illustration's edges clear.
3. Guide cards retain the horizontal swipe cue, arrows and position count on phones.
4. Optional system settings stay collapsed for simple phone and portable enquiries.
5. Result choices show remaining battery and price, with technical ratings kept behind one disclosure.
