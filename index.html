Questions

Things people usually ask.

Still not sure? Chat with us on WhatsApp and we will answer in plain language.

Can it run a fridge?
A mini fridge (under 90 W) can run on the Power Tank, but the compressor surge draws three times the running watts at start-up. A full-size fridge draws too much. Use the calculator to check your specific setup.
How do I recharge it?
Three ways: plug into any mains wall socket (about 2 hours), connect a solar panel up to 400 W (typically 2 to 4 hours in good sunlight), or combine both at once for the fastest refill of about 2 hours. Cables are included.
How long does the battery last before it wears out?
The Power Tank uses LiFePO4 (lithium iron phosphate) chemistry, rated for 6,000+ charge cycles. Charged once a day, that is over 16 years of daily use before capacity noticeably degrades.
Is it safe to use indoors?
Yes. Unlike a petrol generator, it produces no fumes and no carbon monoxide. It runs quietly — under 47 dB. You can use it in a bedroom or office. Keep it away from direct heat sources.
What does the warranty cover?
12 months against manufacturing defects (pending supplier confirmation of extended terms). This does not cover physical damage or misuse. Contact us on WhatsApp to raise a warranty claim and we will guide you through the process.
Do you deliver to my state?
We deliver across Nigeria. Fees vary by state and are shown in the trust section above. Lagos orders typically arrive in 1 to 2 business days; other states take 3 to 5 business days.
How is it different from a home inverter?
A home inverter is fixed, needs installation, separate batteries and wiring. A power station is plug and play — charge it, move it, use it anywhere. No installation cost, no maintenance, lower upfront price.
What if my load is too big for a portable unit?
If the calculator tells you that no portable unit covers your load, your situation calls for a home battery system (a larger battery, a separate inverter and an installer). We carry the Hithium ARKVOLT range for exactly this. Send us a message on WhatsApp and we will work through the options with you.
