## What our homepage does differently, and why it looks worse

### Sunvora

1. A bright, airy page uses one soft background; our heavy hero and black bands fragment it.
2. Clean medium-weight headings lead the page; our bold type and italic serif flourish compete.
3. The framed hero centres its headline; our left-aligned headline sits behind a heavy scrim.
4. An arch-shaped photograph has specification labels around it; our product collage has no comparable focus.
5. Large stat numerals have small quiet labels; our empty comparison values look like unfinished results.
6. Each section changes composition while sharing one surface and radius family; ours repeats label, heading and paragraph.
7. One dark rounded footer closes the page; our full-width dark bands create an unrelated zebra pattern.

### Windora

1. Large rounded containers sit in from the page edge; our sections feel full-width and disconnected.
2. Floating glass cards overlap the hero photograph; our cards sit in a conventional row under the text.
3. Numbered steps create progression; our audience accordion repeats the calculator's first decision.
4. The dashboard groups related controls into one bounded composition; our calculator stretches through many screens.
5. Generous spacing is even; our roughly 200 to 250 pixel gaps vary and break the rhythm.
6. The same soft surfaces and radius family carry from hero to dashboard; our hard black bands interrupt that family.
7. A rounded dark footer anchors the page; ours is a full-width rectangle with a separate closing section.
