# Stage A visual review

Reference study: [REFERENCE_NOTES.md](REFERENCE_NOTES.md).
Comparisons and full-size captures are saved in `.screenshots/` and ignored by Git.

## Calculator compared with Windora

1. The pale inset shell and nested white input panel follow the dashboard reference.
2. Hairline dividers and small labels keep ten controls organised without heavy shadows.
3. The percentage dominates the result, balancing the denser appliance column.
4. The first mobile review exposed misplaced hours controls; explicit grid rows fixed them.
5. The form is taller than the reference dashboard because all ten appliances remain available.

## Result compared with Sunvora

1. The large ink percentage borrows the stat cards' numeral-to-label contrast.
2. The supporting figures use quiet type and borders instead of competing accent cards.
3. A rounded landscape frame separates the product image from the number.
4. The primary action owns the amber fill; supporting actions stay neutral.
5. The supplied Power Tank image is still a promotional collage, so its framing cannot make it a clean cut-out.

## Checkout compared with Windora

1. A pale summary and white form reproduce the reference's nested panel hierarchy.
2. The large heading and small section label preserve the home's type hierarchy.
3. The rounded image frame, fine borders and generous padding match the calculator.
4. Unit price, delivery and total now align clearly, with the total given more scale.
5. The mobile layout stacks the two panels without clipping; cleaner supplier photography remains the visual limitation.

## Verification

The live browser returned Power Go, Power Tank 1000 with a two-hour fan trim, Power Tank, and a whole-home result for audit inputs a to d.
All five audience previews matched their calculator results after loading the preset.
Home, checkout and confirmation had no horizontal overflow at 360, 390 or 1440 pixels.
Lagos showed 285000 plus 3000, and the demo confirmation recorded the same 288000 total.
Missing checkout selections were refused, null-price checkout was hidden, mobile navigation opened and closed, and static reveal content remained visible without JavaScript.

## Deferred by the brief

Full hero, gap, four-product range and footer redesign; the whole-home strip; and server-side payment verification remain for later stages.
WhatsApp still needs the real number, the lead endpoint is empty, and payments remain in demo mode until configuration and server verification are ready.
